⚡ Quick Start Guide (2026 Workflow)

Get up and running in minutes with multi-profile auto-filling, local LaTeX tailoring, and regional compliance packs.

1
Set Up Your Primary Profile & Multi-Profiles Profile Hub
Open the Applicant Profile & Question Bank. Upload your PDF resume to auto-populate personal details, education, and work history. You can create distinct profiles (e.g., Full-Stack Engineer vs AI/ML Engineer) with dedicated colors and target titles.
Pro Tip: Click Clone Profile to duplicate your primary profile and tailor specific skills or summaries without re-entering contact details.
2
Activate Jurisdiction Country-Packs & Employment-Packs Compliance
Navigate to Settings → Country-Packs. Enable regional modules for your job market (India IN, United States US, Germany DE, United Kingdom GB) to standardize local IDs (PAN, SSN, NI, Steuer-ID), university scores, and work authorization. Then enable Employment-Packs to link GitHub, LeetCode, Figma, or Kaggle handles.
3
Choose Your AI Engine: Local Ollama or Cloud BYOK AI Setup
Configure your AI backend in Settings → AI Configuration. Use 100% offline Ollama (Llama 3.2, Mistral, DeepSeek-R1, Qwen2.5) with zero data leaving your machine, or connect directly to OpenAI, Anthropic Claude, Google Gemini, Groq, or OpenRouter using your private API keys stored strictly in browser storage.
Zero Telemetry: Private Apply has no centralized tracking servers. All inference requests route directly to your chosen provider or local Ollama host.
4
1-Click Auto-Fill on Any ATS Job Application Auto-Fill
Navigate to any job application portal (Greenhouse, Lever, Workday, Ashby, Taleo, BambooHR, LinkedIn). Private Apply detects the form and shows a floating action badge. Click Auto-Fill to populate personal information, country pack fields, employment links, and custom questions.
5
Tailor Your LaTeX Résumé Locally LaTeX Engine
Open the LaTeX Résumé Tailor. Paste the job description to run an instant ATS keyword match analysis. The AI aligns your bullet points to the target role while preserving your LaTeX formatting. The local companion daemon compiles the .tex source into a crisp PDF directly on your laptop.
6
Track Applications & Sync via Encrypted Secret Gist Zero-Cloud Sync
Submitted applications automatically log to the Application Dashboard Kanban board. To sync profiles and tracker history across your personal computers without trusting a cloud SaaS, enable GitHub Secret Gist Sync with end-to-end encryption in Settings.

❓ Frequently Anticipated Questions

Answers to common questions regarding navigation, multi-profiles, local companion daemon, country packs, AI engines, and synchronization.

Workspace Navigation & Single-Tab Architecture
Why do pages stay in the same tab instead of opening new tabs?▼
Private Apply uses a unified in-place navigation workflow. In older versions, clicking links created a new browser tab for every page, leading to duplicate tabs (e.g. multiple Home tabs or multiple Dashboard tabs). Now, internal pages (Home, Dashboard, Profiles, Settings, Help, LaTeX Tailor, etc.) navigate in-place while preserving your browser history. You can use the top Back button, Home button, or your browser's back button (Alt + ←) without cluttering your browser with duplicate tabs.
How do I return to the Home page or go back from any page?▼
Every workspace screen features two persistent header buttons:
  • Back: Takes you directly to the previous page in your session history.
  • Home: Instantly returns you to the main Application Workspace dashboard (home/home.html).
Multi-Profile Management
How do I create and switch between multiple applicant profiles?▼
Open the Applicant Profile page (profiles/profiles.html). At the top, you'll see your profile selector bar with active profile badges.
  • Click + New Profile to create an empty profile with a custom target role and color theme.
  • Click Clone to copy all existing education, work history, and contact details into a new profile variant.
  • Click Set as Default to choose which profile is automatically activated when filling new job application forms.
You can also switch the active profile directly from the extension popup with one click before auto-filling.
Can different profiles have different resumes or skills?▼
Yes! Each profile is completely independent. For example, your Full-Stack Engineer profile can highlight React and Node.js, while your AI/ML Engineer profile highlights PyTorch, CUDA, and LLMs. Auto-fill, Cover Letter generation, and Resume Tailoring will always draw exclusively from the currently active profile.
Jurisdiction Country-Packs & Compliance Fields
Which Country-Packs are supported and what fields do they include?▼
Private Apply includes built-in jurisdiction modules:
  • India (IN-Pack): Permanent Account Number (PAN), Aadhaar, UAN/EPFO, 10th & 12th Board marks, CGPA/CPI, College Tier, Backlog history, and University Roll Number.
  • United States (US-Pack): Masked SSN, Work Authorization (US Citizen, Green Card, OPT/CPT, H1B, STEM OPT), and voluntary Self-ID (Veteran, Disability, Race/Ethnicity).
  • Germany (DE-Pack): Steuer-ID, Arbeitserlaubnis (EU Citizen, Blue Card), German academic grading (1.0–5.0 scale), and Kündigungsfrist (Notice Period).
  • United Kingdom (GB-Pack): National Insurance Number (NI), Right to Work (Settled Status, Skilled Worker, Graduate Route), and UK Degree classification (1st, 2:1, 2:2).
Enable or disable packs under Settings → Country-Packs.
Are sensitive fields like SSN or PAN protected?▼
Yes. Sensitive identifiers are assigned a Confidential trust tier. They are masked by default in the UI (showing only the last 4 digits) and require an explicit unmask click to view. When synced via Secret Gist, they are encrypted with AES-GCM encryption. They are never sent to external servers.
Local LaTeX Résumé Tailoring & Companion Daemon
How does the LaTeX Tailor compile TeX into PDF locally?▼
Private Apply connects to a lightweight local companion daemon running on http://127.0.0.1:5005. The daemon compiles LaTeX directly on your computer using either Tectonic (recommended, self-contained) or system pdflatex/xelatex. This guarantees your resume is rendered instantly without sending sensitive career history to remote compilation servers.
How do I start the LaTeX Companion Daemon?▼
If you have Node.js or Python installed, run the companion daemon script included in the extension:
npm run companion # or: python3 native-host/latex-daemon.py
Once running, the LaTeX Tailor editor will show a green Daemon Connected indicator and automatically live-render your PDF as you edit.
Can I use the Résumé Tailor without LaTeX or without the companion daemon?▼
Yes! If the daemon is not running, the Résumé Tailor offers a Plain Text / Markdown mode. You can still perform full ATS keyword gap analysis, synthesize tailored bullet points, and copy your optimized content directly into Google Docs or Word.
Multi-AI Providers & Offline Privacy
What is the difference between Ollama and Cloud AI providers?▼
  • Ollama (Local Offline): Runs open-source language models (Llama 3.2, Mistral, Qwen 2.5, DeepSeek-R1) directly on your laptop's GPU/CPU. It requires zero API keys, costs $0, and functions with complete offline privacy.
  • Cloud Providers (BYOK): Direct connections to OpenAI (GPT-4o), Anthropic (Claude 3.5 Sonnet), Google Gemini, Groq (Llama-3.3-70B), or OpenRouter. Recommended if your laptop has limited RAM or if you prefer frontier cloud models. Your API keys are stored in encrypted browser local storage and communicated directly to the respective provider's HTTPS endpoint.
Why is Ollama showing "Disconnected" or not responding?▼
Follow this quick checklist:
  1. Verify that Ollama is running in your terminal: ollama serve (or the Ollama menu bar icon).
  2. Ensure you have pulled at least one model: ollama run llama3.2 or ollama run mistral.
  3. Check CORS permissions: if your browser blocks localhost requests, launch Ollama with OLLAMA_ORIGINS="*" ollama serve.
  4. Check Settings → AI Configuration to run the integrated connection diagnostic.
Encrypted Secret Gist Sync & Backups
How does Secret Gist Sync work and can anyone else see my data?▼
Secret Gist Sync uses a private GitHub Gist owned entirely by your personal GitHub account. Before any profile data or application records are uploaded, they are encrypted client-side using AES-GCM encryption with your chosen secret passphrase. Even GitHub cannot view your resume or job submissions.
How does conflict reconciliation work between two computers?▼
Every profile update includes a cryptographic content hash and a UTC timestamp. When syncing across multiple laptops, Private Apply compares the remote Gist timestamp against your local record. If an edit is detected on both sides, the extension notifies you and gives you the option to merge records, accept the newer version, or retain your local copy.
Can I export and import my data without using GitHub?▼
Yes! On the Home page sidebar under Data Management, or on the Dashboard, you can click Export Profile (JSON) and Export CSV to save your complete workspace data locally to your Downloads folder at any time.
